Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

MD OMOR FARUK operates Rose & Rowan: Candy Garden. Contact me about privacy at ofsomor1@gmail.com.

This policy covers the game and its privacy and support website. I intend the game for children and adults. The current pre-release build keeps production advertising switched off. Developer test builds can display Google sample ads.

Saved progress

The game saves your puzzle and preferences in app storage on your device. It uses these records to resume play, choose puzzles and keep the token balance consistent.

  • Puzzle positions, Undo history, time remaining, level progress and difficulty choices.
  • Token earnings and spending, gift claims, ad-reward receipts and purchase or refund records.
  • Sound, music, vibration and voice preferences, plus tutorial progress and ad-break timing.

You can play the core puzzles offline. The game has no player account or publisher-operated gameplay server. I receive information you choose to send through support.

Apple may include app data in device backups under your settings. Deleting app data or changing devices can remove progress and tokens. The current local wallet has no publisher-managed cross-device recovery service.

Purchases and tokens

Apple handles checkout when token packs are available. Buying requires internet access. The app receives purchase identifiers, product details and verification or refund information to deliver tokens and prevent duplicate credit.

Apple handles your payment details under its privacy terms. You can read those terms and request a refund through Apple.

Apple privacy information · Apple refund help

The game keeps transaction and spending records with the local save. Contact support before removing app data if a purchase has failed to arrive.

Advertising

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Production ads are disabled in the current release configuration. Test builds can contact Google to load sample ads.

During ad use, Google may process device or app identifiers, network information such as IP address, diagnostics, performance data and ad views or interactions. Google describes these categories in its SDK disclosure. Sample ads still involve network requests.

Our ad code applies child-directed treatment and a general-audience content limit. It disables publisher personalisation, the publisher first-party identifier and the Mobile Ads coarse-location setting. The consent request marks users as under the age of consent. These controls leave other Google service processing subject to Google's terms and privacy information.

We will review this policy, the children's privacy requirements and the final provider settings before enabling production ads. Ad privacy choices appear in Settings when Google's service requires them. Parents can report an ad through Support.

Google privacy policy · Google ad SDK data disclosure

Children and parents

The game asks for no date of birth. Rose and Rowan use recorded voices, and you can play without recording your voice.

A parent or guardian can contact ofsomor1@gmail.com about a child's information, ask to review or delete information held through support, or raise a concern about a service provider. I may ask for enough information to verify the request and the parent's authority. Please start with the issue description and avoid sending a child's name, photograph or other identifying details.

I will assess any planned collection of children's information before introducing it, including the notice and parental consent required for that use.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or embedded social media. GitHub processes hosting information under its own privacy statement.

GitHub privacy statement

If you email support, I receive your email address, message and attachments. I use them to answer the request, investigate the issue and handle any privacy complaint. Google provides the Gmail mailbox and processes messages under its privacy terms.

I delete support correspondence within 30 days after resolving a request, unless I need to retain a record for a legal obligation or an active dispute. Please remove payment details and children's identifying information from screenshots.

Retention and service providers

The device and its backup settings control how long local game records remain. Apple, Google and GitHub retain their own service records under their policies and legal duties. They may process information in countries outside your own. Their linked privacy information describes their handling of that information and available contact routes.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can change game preferences in Settings. You can ask me to access, correct or delete information I hold through support, or raise a privacy complaint, at ofsomor1@gmail.com. Your rights and the response period depend on the law that applies to your request.

I can handle the information I hold. Device-local game data remains on your device and in any backups you control. Apple, Google and GitHub provide routes for requests about information they hold.

You may also contact your local privacy regulator. In Australia, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Australian privacy complaint information

Security

The app verifies Apple purchase records, checks for duplicate credits and uses protected save writes. Modified devices, restored backups and deleted app data can affect a local wallet's integrity or recovery.

Changes to this policy

I will update the date on this page when the policy changes. Before changing children's data processing, I will assess the notice and parental permission that the change requires.
