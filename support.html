Common questions

Sound, voices and vibration

Check Music, Move Sounds, Celebration Voice and Vibration in the game's Settings. Raise your media volume and check whether Bluetooth headphones still have the audio connection. You can hear enabled game sounds through the speaker in Silent Mode. On a supported iPhone, you should feel a light haptic when you select a candy and land on a legal destination.

Replay and Start from beginning

Replay on the win screen restarts the completed puzzle for free. Start from beginning on the main menu opens Level 1. It keeps your token balance, gift records, purchases and previously earned milestone rewards.

Daily gifts after a missed day

A missed full local calendar day resets your next claim to Day 1. Claim Monday, skip Tuesday, return Wednesday and you receive the Day 1 gift. You keep the tokens you earned before the gap.

Tokens after an ad

Completing an optional rewarded ad earns two tokens. Skipping, cancelling or failing to load an ad earns zero. Banner and level-break ads earn zero. The first seven levels remain ad-free. Keep the app open if it asks you to retry saving an earned reward.

A token purchase has not arrived

Reopen the game with internet access and check for a purchase-delivery or save-retry message. Contact support before buying the same pack again. Share the product name, purchase date and Apple's transaction reference if needed. Keep your payment-card details private.

Apple handles refund requests through its own service.

Apple refund help

Saved progress or tokens are missing

Contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app. The game keeps progress and tokens in local app storage. Removing that data or switching devices can lose access to the current balance. The current app has no publisher-managed cross-device wallet recovery service.

A save warning appears

Keep the existing app data and use the recovery or retry control shown in the game. Contact support with the warning text and a screenshot. You can leave the warning visible while you ask for help.

Report an inappropriate ad

Send the time, app version and level number, plus a screenshot if you can take one. Include the advertiser name or ad details shown by the ad's information control. Avoid opening the advertiser's website to investigate.

Email ofsomor1@gmail.com. A parent or guardian can report on a child's behalf.
