How to play

Use the row and column counts to plan your moves. Place candies on rings. Crosses mark walls, and walls and other candies block a move.

Rose and Rowan guide you through the puzzles. Work through 240 campaign boards, then try more in Endless mode.

A puzzle for your next break

Play timed boards with two to six minutes on the clock. You can play the core puzzles offline.
